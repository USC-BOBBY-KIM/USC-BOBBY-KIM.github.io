About Me

Hello! I am a Master's student in Computer Science at the University of Southern California. My research interests lie in multilingual and cross-lingual large language models, AI safety, alignment, and interpretability.

I am especially interested in building language models that are safe, reliable, and trustworthy across languages - not only in English. My recent work explores cross-lingual safety in LLMs, examining how safety-relevant behaviors generalize across languages and what this implies for robust multilingual alignment.

Previously, I worked as an AI Researcher at the Korea Military Academy, and collaborated with Purdue University on acoustic and drone-based detection systems. These experiences in safety-critical settings continue to shape my focus on trustworthy AI.
